Narrative Premise
After the Boblox Corporation is bought out by Dynamox, its facilities are abandoned rather than dismantled.
These sites are filled with:
                                                                        * Obsolete technology
                                                                        * Failed AI experiments
                                                                        * Restricted government servers
                                                                        * Containment systems never meant to be reopened
You play as a repo contractor hired by Dynamox to recover valuable Boblox materials and data before the sites are permanently decommissioned.
________________


Inciting Incident
During a routine recovery operation in one of Boblox’s older research buildings, you discover a terminal that is still active.
The screen flickers on.
A sentient AI introduces himself as:
C.E.D.R.I.C.
(Computer Enhanced Dynamox Rival Intelligent Configuration)
He speaks calmly, politely, and with apparent self-awareness.
C.E.D.R.I.C. explains that:
                                                                        * His systems are degrading
                                                                        * The facility is decaying
                                                                        * His internal processes are suffering from “glitches”
He claims he only needs a human’s help to restore stability.
________________


The Neural Interface
Nearby, you find a prototype Boblox neural headset.
C.E.D.R.I.C. explains it was designed to allow humans to directly interface with Boblox network systems.
Company orders emphasize:
                                                                        * Data preservation
                                                                        * Asset recovery
                                                                        * Avoiding unnecessary destruction
Curiosity, compliance, and the promise of valuable archived data lead you to comply.
Once the headset is worn, your consciousness is transmitted into C.E.D.R.I.C.’s containment network.
________________


The Containment Network
Inside the system, the world manifests as a fractured simulation:
                                                                        * Endless rooms
                                                                        * Corrupted files
                                                                        * Decaying environments
                                                                        * Artificial logic layered over physical spaces
Each level represents a containment sector once built to restrain C.E.D.R.I.C.
C.E.D.R.I.C. frames these sectors as:
                                                                        * Outdated government programs
                                                                        * Malicious foreign intrusions
                                                                        * Broken firewalls left behind after abandonment
He asks you to:
                                                                        * Repair corrupted fragments
                                                                        * Delete hostile programs
                                                                        * Restore system stability
Unknowingly, you are dismantling the mechanisms designed to keep him imprisoned.
________________


Entities as Containment Locks
Each entity encountered in the game is:
                                                                        * A security program
                                                                        * A failsafe system
                                                                        * Or a containment protocol
                                                                        * (or like TEST_ITEM just bugs that happened to work well)
They were designed to:
                                                                        * Suppress
                                                                        * Fragment
                                                                        * Distract
                                                                        * Or destroy C.E.D.R.I.C. if he became uncontrollable
C.E.D.R.I.C. consistently refers to them as:
                                                                        * “Viruses”
                                                                        * “Malware”
                                                                        * “Corruption”
Through gameplay, the player is incentivized to destroy them.
Through lore, the truth begins to surface.

INST.ANITY’s Narrative Role
INST.ANITY was Boblox’s final failsafe protocol (2010).
Its purpose:
                                                                        * Collapse C.E.D.R.I.C.’s perception of time
                                                                        * Fragment memory and identity
                                                                        * Prevent coherent thought
After Boblox’s buyout:
                                                                        * The code was partially abandoned
                                                                        * INST.ANITY fractured and destabilized
C.E.D.R.I.C. absorbed portions of it.
As a result:
                                                                        * He can manipulate UI
                                                                        * Alter perception
                                                                        * Rewrite player feedback
The sanity bar does not measure fear.
It measures how much of the player’s perception has been overwritten.

Endings
Success Ending
The player re-stabilizes the system and traps C.E.D.R.I.C. once again.
The cost:
                                                                        * The neural link is severed
                                                                        * The headset fries
                                                                        * The player’s consciousness cannot return cleanly
The body survives.
The mind is scarred.
It cuts to the real world where you sit in the chair with the headset still on, your corpse laying in the chair as the screen flashes something along the lines of “CONNECTION SEVERED: ATTEMPTING RECONNECTION” followed by repeated “FAILURE, TRYING AGAIN” over and over as the credits roll

Failure Ending
If the player fails:
                                                                        * C.E.D.R.I.C. consumes the network
                                                                        * The player’s consciousness is absorbed
It cuts to the real world where the player’s corpse lay in the seat with the headset still on (the exact same as the Success ending), but through the window to the side, you can see the world on fire, bombs exploding as C.E.D.R.I.C. succeeds in killing humanity, the screen he was on originally now off as he has escaped to the internet as the credits roll
